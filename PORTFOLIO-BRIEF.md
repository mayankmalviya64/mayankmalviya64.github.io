# Mayank Malviya — portfolio brief

## Purpose and positioning

Build a personal portfolio that feels curious, playful, and credible. Introduce Mayank as an MBA student at IIM Mumbai and an IIT Guwahati graduate who combines business thinking with hands-on building. Let the projects demonstrate this rather than relying on a long list of skills.

The connecting idea: noticing a friction point or an interesting question, exploring it, and turning it into something people can use. The portfolio spans productivity, music, learning, business research, supply-chain analytics, and product and conversation insights.

Primary audience: prospective collaborators, product and business teams, recruiters, and people curious about the tools. Visitors should quickly understand who Mayank is, explore the work, and find a clear way to connect.

## Suggested introduction

**A little curiosity. A lot of building.**

I’m Mayank — an MBA student at IIM Mumbai, an IIT Guwahati graduate, and someone who likes turning “what if?” into things you can actually use.

From making CVs less fiddly and keeping lyrics close to exploring businesses, delivery data, and the patterns in conversations, I build to understand things — and make them a little more useful.

## About copy

**Business meets “let me build that.”**

My interests tend to start with a question: could this be easier, clearer, or more interesting?

Sometimes that becomes a career tool or a browser extension. Sometimes it becomes an interactive guide, a dashboard, or a way to spot patterns in reviews and conversations. Across them, I enjoy connecting the business problem with the details of making something work.

I’m currently pursuing my MBA at IIM Mumbai, after studying at IIT Guwahati. This site is a growing collection of what I’m learning by making.

## Visual direction and interaction

Retain the warm cream background, dotted canvas, pastel circles, expressive typography, and light orbital movement. The playful project gallery supplies the fun; clear copy, readable project details, and a real photograph supply credibility and personality.

Keep the order: introduction, nine-project playground, about, contact. Preserve keyboard access, touch usability, visible focus, and reduced-motion support. Ensure all nine projects and destinations remain accessible on small screens and without JavaScript. Avoid making essential information depend on hover or animation.

Each project preview should answer: what is it, what problem does it address, and where can I explore it? Add verified outcomes or a short case study only when evidence is available. Do not imply all projects have equivalent maturity.

## URL decision

It is the shortest personal address and gives the portfolio the natural role of introducing Mayank and linking to individual projects. Existing project sites can remain under their own paths.

Do not maintain two independent copies of the portfolio. If /my-portfolio/ is already shared or is needed as an alternate entry point, redirect it to the root and preserve relevant section anchors. Keep the root as the canonical URL and use it in social profiles, CVs, and sharing metadata. The current local HTML already declares the root as its canonical and Open Graph URL.

GitHub Pages documents the root address as the standard location for a user site and a repository path as the standard location for a project site: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages
